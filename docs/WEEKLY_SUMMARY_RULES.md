# Weekly Summary Rules

Internal boundaries use timezone-aware UTC. A week starts Monday 00:00 UTC and ends seven days later. Display timezone remains future configuration work.

Private sections may include episodes aired, missing episodes, starts, finishes, movies, completed server titles, open reviews, refresh failures, and upcoming titles. Shared sections are limited to new episodes added, seasons completed, movies added, and new series available. Empty sections are omitted.

Week start, channel purpose, and summary type form the deduplication identity. Private and shared formats therefore remain independent. Long summaries split on item boundaries, keep the most important content, and retain silent policy on every shared part. Milestone 6 generates and enqueues summaries but does not alter the Sunday scheduler.
